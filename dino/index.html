How to play

Jump

Press Space or ↑, tap the screen, or hit the A button to leap over cacti, boulders and lava.

Duck

Hold ↓ or the B button to slide under the low-flying birds.

Breathe fire

Season 2: grab a glowing orb for 3 fireballs, then press Ctrl (or F, or the F button). They bounce along the ground and burn birds and small cacti. Big saguaros, boulders and meteors still need a jump.

Beat the crown

Climb the leaderboard and try to pass my best run. Add your name when you set a new high score.
